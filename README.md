### Decision Defence System

The **Decision Defence System** is the practical system layer for structured decision-making.

It focuses on:

1. Defining the decision
2. Identifying the evidence required
3. Evaluating information quality
4. Comparing alternatives
5. Identifying uncertainty and assumptions
6. Making the decision
7. Translating the decision into execution
8. Maintaining accountability and verification

# AI–Human Interaction

My work examines the relationship between human judgement and increasingly capable AI systems.

Key areas include:

* Human agency
* Decision responsibility
