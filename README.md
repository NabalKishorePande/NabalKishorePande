🧠 Key Research Projects & Frameworks
My research has led to the development of original decision systems. Here are some of the key projects I am currently working on:
Opportunity Intelligence Framework™: A five-step decision model to help students choose the right educational paths without feeling overwhelmed by too many choices. (Hosted on Zenodo)
Execution Compression System (ECS): A constraint-based framework designed to reduce decision load and improve execution reliability in governance systems. (Hosted on Zenodo)
NeuroGenesis Framework: An open research project exploring how human thinking and AI work together to make learning faster and boost mental productivity. (Hosted on OSF)
AI-Driven Workforce Reorganization (2026–2031): A working paper analyzing institutional projections on automation, job displacement, and skills volatility. (Hosted on OSF)
Other notable frameworks include: Executive Decision Defense Playbook™, Opportunity Debt, Research Production System™, The Academic Word Engine, The ASA System, Funding Command Centre, and the Fit Matrix.
